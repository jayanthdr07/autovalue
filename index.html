About AutoValue AI

Understanding valuation drivers and machine learning integration architecture.

Why Vehicle Parameters Matter

  • Brand & Model: Establishes market demand tier, spare availability, and brand resale hold rate.
  • Age & Depreciation: Vehicle values drop steepest in years 1–3, stabilizing after year 5.
  • Kilometres Driven: Direct reflection of mechanical wear on engine, gearbox, and suspension.
  • Fuel Type & Transmission: Automatics and high-efficiency fuels command premium pricing in urban markets.
